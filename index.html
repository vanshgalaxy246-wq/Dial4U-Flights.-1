Why Antigua?

Antigua is known for its coastline of white-sand beaches and clear turquoise water. Sailing harbours, historic sites such as Nelson’s Dockyard and Shirley Heights, and a relaxed island atmosphere define the experience.

Luxury resorts sit alongside quiet coves. Whether you come for the sea, the light or a slower pace, the journey begins with the right flight from the UK.
